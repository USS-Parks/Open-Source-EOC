Map data
--------

OpenStreetMap (when included)
  These files are databases derived from OpenStreetMap:
  - web/public/basemap/california.pmtiles, the street basemap;
  - web/public/basemap/buildings.pmtiles, building footprints;
  - tools/basemap/out/gazetteer.tsv, the address search gazetteer.

  © OpenStreetMap contributors. The data is available under the Open
  Database License 1.0 (ODbL): https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/
  See also https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright. The map shows this
  attribution while the street or building archive is in use.

  Each file above is a whole derived database, conveyed under the ODbL. It
  was made from the Geofabrik extract of California,
  https://download.geofabrik.de/north-america/us/california.html, with the
  build tools in the Open Source EOC source repository:
  tools/basemap/generate-california.sh builds the street and building
  archives with Planetiler, tools/basemap/build-overture-buildings.ps1 adds
  the Overture values below to the building archive, and
  tools/basemap/build-gazetteer.mjs builds the gazetteer from the street
  archive. tools/basemap/README.md in that repository describes each step.

Overture Maps buildings (when included)
  web/public/basemap/buildings.pmtiles carries building use and subtype
  values from the Overture Maps Foundation buildings release named in
  web/public/basemap/buildings-overture.json, matched to OpenStreetMap
  ways. © Overture Maps Foundation. Open Database License 1.0, as above.

California road jurisdiction and public land overlays (when included)
  web/public/basemap/overlays.pmtiles is compiled from public agency map
  services: the Caltrans State Highway Network; USDA Forest Service roads;
  Bureau of Land Management public motorized roads; National Park Service
  public roads; CAL FIRE public land ownership, compiled from the
  California Protected Areas Database and US Fish and Wildlife Service,
  Department of Defense and Bureau of Indian Affairs data; and Humboldt
  County GIS roads. web/public/basemap/overlays-manifest.json records each
  source's address and attribution. The source services declare no reuse
  license, and the terms for redistributing this compilation are under
  review.

Bundled basemap
  web/public/basemap/basemap.pmtiles and ca_counties.geojson come from
  Natural Earth and US Census boundaries, both in the public domain.

Liberation Sans
