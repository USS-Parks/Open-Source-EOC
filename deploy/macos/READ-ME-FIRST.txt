Open Source EOC for macOS: the North Coast Storm demo
macOS 13 or later, Apple silicon or Intel. Synthetic data only.

1. Drag "Open Source EOC" into Applications.

2. The app is not yet signed by an Apple developer, so macOS asks before
   the first open. In Applications, Control-click "Open Source EOC", choose
   Open, then choose Open again. On macOS 15 and later, if there is no Open
   button: open it once, then in System Settings, Privacy & Security, choose
   "Open Anyway".

3. The first open sets up the demo, about two minutes, then opens it in
   Chrome or Edge as an app window, or in your default browser. Later opens
   take seconds. Sign in with the demo accounts listed in
   TRY-IT-ON-WINDOWS.md in the repository; the demo is the same.

4. To stop the demo, run this in Terminal:
   "/Applications/Open Source EOC.app/Contents/MacOS/Open Source EOC" stop

Data lives in ~/Library/Application Support/Open Source EOC and the log in
~/Library/Logs/Open Source EOC. The network host runs on Windows; Macs
reach a host with Safari or Chrome.
