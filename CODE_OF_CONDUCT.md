# Code of Conduct

This project serves emergency managers. The bar is professional conduct,
the same one an EOC floor runs on.

- Be direct about the work; be respectful to the person.
- Critique code, plans, and evidence. Never demean people, and never
  discriminate on any personal characteristic.
- No harassment, in project spaces or directed at project members outside
  them.
- Disagreements are settled with evidence and, when needed, by the
  governance process in GOVERNANCE.md.

Report conduct problems to the lead maintainer (see GOVERNANCE.md).
Reports are handled confidentially. The lead maintainer may warn, restrict,
or remove participants to protect the project and its people.
