# Veoci Platform Research: Fit for Open Source EOC

**Created:** 2026-09-24
**Author of record:** Basho Parks (research compiled by a Claude Code session)
**Purpose:** Profiles Veoci (Grey Wall Software, LLC, trading as Veoci, New Haven CT): its company and commercial model, its integrated building blocks (rooms, forms, workflows, tasks, plans, custom actions, dashboards, maps, notification), its packages (Veoci Core / CORE Gov, COOP Gov, AIM Gov and the vertical lines), its API, single sign-on, contact lists and alerting path, its Whole Community Solutions, and its implementation claims. Scores every capability for fit in Open Source EOC, weighted toward a small tribal or rural EOC, with county and state notes. Written to inform future rosters.
**Status:** RESEARCH COMPLETE, evidence-limited (see the first section). This document is not a roster and authorizes no work. The candidate work items VC-01 to VC-39 are candidates only; each needs a Basho-approved roster or amendment before execution.
**Baseline:** the repository was read at `c6b3e42`. `main` has since moved to `023beaa` with design-fidelity screen work and a lifeline assessment follow-up field; no row below depends on those changes.
**Relation to other documents:** a companion to [VIRTUAL-EOC-PLATFORM-RESEARCH-2026-09-17.md](./VIRTUAL-EOC-PLATFORM-RESEARCH-2026-09-17.md), the research basis behind the roster, which it does not supersede. Veoci was not profiled there. Capability status cited below is the status in [VEOC-PARITY-MATRIX.md](../VEOC-PARITY-MATRIX.md) and receipts are cited by heading in [V1-LEDGER.md](./V1-LEDGER.md).

## Summary

Veoci is a hosted, per-user-licensed no-code platform from a Connecticut company of about 110 people (Grey Wall Software, trading as Veoci) whose six building blocks (rooms, forms, workflows, tasks, plans and custom actions, plus dashboards, maps and a notification engine) are sold as vertical packages such as Veoci Core (CORE Gov), COOP Gov and AIM Gov, with implementation, integration and configuration sold as services. **Open Source EOC already matches or exceeds most of the pieces that matter to an EOC**: incident-scoped boards, versioned no-code schemas, a workflow engine with approvals and escalation, call-down notification with acknowledgement, ICS and FEMA outputs, offline field capture, a map-first common operating picture, and three things Veoci shows no evidence of at all: native standards interchange, an IPAWS send path and instance-to-instance federation. The Veoci ideas worth taking for a Yurok-sized tribal EOC are the ones that cut configuration labor for a thin staff: **executable plans** that launch an incident with tasks and notifications in one action, a declarative action catalog, poll-style replies and voice or SMS-reply call-down through a separate telephony service, signatures and QR tags, FEMA Public Assistance force account capture, continuity (COOP) templates, and signed starter packs delivered with a rollout playbook and validated imports. Veoci's public forms, public map dashboards (Honolulu has linked them in official alerts since July 2025), resident opt-in alerting and any scripting collide with the For Official Use Only decision and ADR-0004; those are Basho's decisions, and this report states what changing each would take rather than recommending it. **The largest caveat is evidence quality**: the environment's egress policy blocked veoci.com, fema.gov, dhs.gov, the FedRAMP marketplace, the review sites and most procurement hosts, so most Veoci facts below are search-result text and carry a confidence grade, and "not found" never means "confirmed absent". OpenEOC's own claims are CI evidence on synthetic data: it has no pilot, no live activation and no timed onboarding, and on that axis Veoci, with contracts such as Alameda County's (about $166 per user per year) and Austin's ($3.421 million ceiling), is plainly ahead.

## Most Veoci facts here are search-result text, so each carries a grade

The research environment refused direct page reads for veoci.com, static.veoci.com, fema.gov, dhs.gov, marketplace.fedramp.gov, g2.com, capterra.com, gartner.com, peerspot.com, trustradius.com, apps.apple.com, play.google.com, sam.gov, usaspending.gov, govtribe.com, highergov.com, dir.texas.gov, austintexas.gov, alamedacountyca.gov and most other procurement hosts; help.veoci.com did not resolve, and the shared budget of 200 web searches ran out before several planned searches (task recurrence, audit trail, dashboards, retention, the CodeRED and AlertMedia integrations) could run. **Almost every Veoci statement in this report therefore comes from a search engine's summary of the cited page**, not from reading it; quoted wording is close to verbatim but unverified. Two kinds of evidence were read directly and are the strongest available: public GitHub code from Veoci's developers (the 2023 front-end prototype [GreywallSoftware/vuetify-test](https://github.com/GreywallSoftware/vuetify-test), which targets `stage.veoci.com` with `@veoci.com` test users, and the 2025 to 2026 [veoci-labs](https://github.com/veoci-labs/veoci-solution-mapper) repositories, which call `veoci.com/api/v2` and appear to be Veoci engineering's), and the LICENSE files of every open-source project named. The OpenEOC baseline was read from the repository at `main` `c6b3e42`, read-only.

Confidence grades used throughout: **H** means an independent, dated record (a government document, court record or customer post) or first-party code read directly; **M** means a Veoci page as shown in search text, which is a vendor claim in paraphrase; **L** means a search summary with uncertain page attribution, an inference from field names or URL shapes, or a single unverified summary. When an OpenEOC capability reads "verified", it carries the parity matrix's meaning: a CI-green implementation gate met at the depth assessed, on synthetic data, "not a commercial-parity certification" (`docs/VEOC-PARITY-MATRIX.md`). The reads that would most improve this report are the two 2026 FEMA IPAWS alert-origination provider lists ([2026-05-18](https://www.fema.gov/sites/default/files/documents/fema_continuity_ipaws-capabilities-alert-origination-software-providers_05182026.pdf)), the Veoci entry in the [DHS SAVER January 2022 market survey](https://www.dhs.gov/sites/default/files/2022-01/SAVER%20IMS%20MSR_05Jan2022-508.pdf), Veoci's gated [Whole Community capability list](https://veoci.com/downloadable/veoci-whole-community-capabilities-list/) and [integrations sheet](https://veoci.com/downloadable/integrations/), and the [Texas DIR-CPO-5533](https://dir.texas.gov/contracts/dir-cpo-5533) Appendix C price index.

## Veoci is six primitives sold as vertical packages plus services

### A 110-person hosted vendor priced per user

Veoci is the trade name of **Grey Wall Software, LLC of New Haven, Connecticut**, founded about 2010 to 2011 by Sukh Grewal and colleagues from GE's SupportCentral project ([Veoci team page](https://veoci.com/about/meet-the-team); [GovTribe](https://govtribe.com/vendors/grey-wall-software-llc-veoci-dot-com-6d1j7)); a court record describes the LLC as "the sole owner of Veoci Inc." ([CourtListener](https://www.courtlistener.com/opinion/10643077/grey-wall-software-llc-v-aerosimple-llc/)), which has signed newer contracts since about 2024. The CEO describes the company as bootstrapped, with about **$15 million revenue and 110 staff in 2024** ([Latka](https://getlatka.com/companies/veoci)), while Accel-KKR lists it as a credit (debt) portfolio company ([Accel-KKR](https://www.accel-kkr.com/portfolio-company/veoci/)). The product is **SaaS on Amazon Web Services** across availability zones, presumably multi-tenant ([Veoci, 2021](https://veoci.com/blog/veoci-iso-27001-security-certification)); no on-premise, GovCloud or disconnected edition was found. Its attestations are ISO 27001 (2022 edition announced December 2025) ([Veoci](https://veoci.com/blog/veoci-iso-27001-2022)), a SOC 2 Type II claim ([VIA FAQ](https://veoci.com/via-ai/via-faq)) and the 2023 TX-RAMP certification above; no FedRAMP or GovRAMP evidence surfaced, and the registries could not be opened.

There is no price list. The best public figure is **Alameda County OES**: an 850-user cap, $140,856.25 for the one-year extension from 2025-12-23 to 2026-12-23, and a seven-year not-to-exceed of $1,064,000, with global SMS send and receive included and Homeland Security Grant Program money paying for it ([Alameda County board letter](https://www.alamedacountyca.gov/board/bos_calendar/documents/DocsAgendaReg_03_17_26/PUBLIC%20PROTECTION/Regular%20Calendar/Sheriff_400825.pdf)). That works out to **about $166 per user per year** including training and SMS (an inference from the amendment structure, grade H for the inputs). The **City of Austin** authorized up to five years at a not-to-exceed of **$3,421,000** on 2024-09-12, after raising an earlier Grey Wall contract to $613,000 in 2022 ([Austin council, 2024-09-12](https://www.austintexas.gov/council/2024/20240912-reg); [2022-03-24](https://www.austintexas.gov/department/city-council/2022/20220324-reg.htm)). The Texas DIR contract lists implementation, integration, configuration, project management and training as service lines beside the SaaS licence ([Texas DIR](https://dir.texas.gov/contracts/dir-cpo-5533)). Oregon shows the channel that reaches small governments: the state bought a statewide continuity licence and offers it "at no cost to eligible organizations", with HSGP funding the "local/tribal access" ([Oregon ODEM](https://www.oregon.gov/oem/emresources/plans_assessments/coop/pages/veoci.aspx)).

Veoci's independent standing is modest. Capterra shows **4.6 of 5 from about 10 reviews** ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/150183/Veoci/reviews/)); PeerSpot's critical-event-management mindshare fell to 3.5% in July 2026 from 7.1% a year earlier, behind Everbridge and WebEOC ([PeerSpot](https://www.peerspot.com/products/comparisons/veoci_vs_webeoc)); its analyst credential is a 2019 Gartner Magic Quadrant Leader placement for business continuity, as Veoci reports it ([Veoci press](https://veoci.com/press/veoci-named-a-leader-in-the-magic-quadrant-for-business-continuity-management-program-solutions-worldwide)). Tennessee's emergency management agency demonstrated Veoci, CloudEOC and DisasterLAN in 2019 to 2020 and **stayed on WebEOC** ([Tennessee fiscal review](https://capitol.tn.gov/Archives/Joint/committees/fiscal-review/contracts/4.%20TEMA%20(ESi%20Aquisition,%20Inc.-%20contract).pdf)). Real public-sector use is documented at Alameda County (since 2019), Oregon, Austin, Irvine, Tulsa, the City of Los Angeles (its Super Bowl LVI after-action report records Veoci for information sharing ([LA EMD AAR/IP](https://ens.lacity.org/epd/eoboldagendas/epdeobagendas210168522_01252023.pdf))), LAUSD, Honolulu, Palo Alto and Trumbull. **No tribal nation was found using Veoci directly**; Oregon's statewide COOP platform is the only tribal link.

### "Veoci Core" is the government EOC package; two of the brief's names do not exist

"Veoci Core connects stakeholders, makes data actionable and interoperable, and centralizes everything in your EOC, from planning and emergencies to daily operations" is Veoci's own wording for its **government EOC package**, styled CORE Gov or COREgov ([CORE Gov](https://veoci.com/government/core-gov/); [CORE](https://veoci.com/core-solutions-for-government/)). Its named contents are one-click activation (launch a response room, notify contacts, auto-assign tasks and checklists), an IAP builder with ICS forms, damage assessment, asset and inventory cost capture, SMS, email, phone and mobile notification, and GIS mapping (grade M). **COOP Gov** is continuity of government that is "integrated with COREgov or other response solutions" ([COOP Gov](https://veoci.com/government/coop-gov)); **AIM Gov** is asset and inventory management ([AIM Gov](https://veoci.com/government/asset-inventory-management/)). Beside them sit Flagship for higher education ([Flagship](https://veoci.com/higher-education/veoci-flagship/)), Vitals EM for healthcare (launched June 2023) ([Veoci press](https://veoci.com/press/veoci-vitals-em-healthcare-emergency-management)), the VIA AI layer, **Whole Community Solutions** for municipal departments ([capability list](https://veoci.com/downloadable/veoci-whole-community-capabilities-list/)), and aviation, utilities, finance continuity and K-12 lines. Every package appears to be a configuration of the same platform: the same primitives recur on every solution page and in every training course, and no tier matrix or per-package price was found. **"Coordination and Tracking" and "Planning and Operations" did not appear as Veoci names in any of three passes**; this report uses them only as groupings of features.

### Rooms, forms, workflows, tasks, plans and custom actions compose every solution

A **Room** is the container for everything: forms, workflows, task types, plans, chat and dashboard tiles, with "Side Rooms" for sub-operations and membership by invitation ([no-code platform](https://veoci.com/veoci-no-code-platform/); [CPP guide](https://www.cpp.edu/em/docs/business-continuity-planning/getting-started-guide-veoci.pdf)). In Veoci's code a room is `containerType: 'MUCROOM'` and its ID doubles as its chat room ID, which suggests rooms began as chat rooms that grew object libraries (grade H for the code, L for the history). A **Form** is single-sitting data collection whose records are entries; field types seen in first-party code include `TEXT`, `NUMERIC`, `FILE`, `LOCATION`, `MAPLINESTRING`, a signature field, `PAYMENT`, a Contact Picker and single-choice fields, each with `mandatory`, `unique` and a `rules` array, and reference fields `REFERENCE`, `FORM_ENTRY`, `LOOKUP`, `WORKFLOW`, `WORKFLOW_LOOKUP` and `TASK` that can point into other rooms ([fieldMocks.js](https://github.com/GreywallSoftware/vuetify-test/blob/main/veoci-web/test/unit/__mocks__/data/fieldMocks.js); [solution mapper analyzer](https://github.com/veoci-labs/veoci-solution-mapper/blob/main/src/veoci_mapper/analyzer.py)). No polygon field type turned up. A **Workflow** is a multi-step form: each step carries an order, `transitions`, a `requireAllConditions` flag and per-step field permissions, and a final step can close the entry ([stepPermissionsMock.js](https://github.com/GreywallSoftware/vuetify-test/blob/main/veoci-web/test/unit/__mocks__/data/stepPermissionsMock.js); [ISU drill](https://veoci.com/blog/school-reunification-drill-isu)); SLAs, escalation, parallel branches and sub-workflows were not confirmed. **Tasks** come from admin-built Task Types with their own field schemas, and assignees can act on a task from email ([CPP guide](https://www.cpp.edu/em/docs/business-continuity-planning/getting-started-guide-veoci.pdf)).

The **Plan** is the composition primitive. Veoci's plan builder stores `planType` values `INCIDENT_RESPONSE` and `RECURRING_EVENT`, an `eventXDate` anchor, a launch message, room-admin rules (`makeLauncherAdmin`, `addParentAdmins`), members, pre-created form entries, custom-action invocations, and task templates with milestones, launch order, delayed release (`secondsUntilLaunch`), hide-until-launch and parent-child nesting ([planBuilderMock.js](https://github.com/GreywallSoftware/vuetify-test/blob/main/veoci-web/test/unit/__mocks__/data/planBuilderMock.js), 2022 mock, grade H for fields and L for semantics). Launched, a plan creates the room, notifies members and generates the task list: "launch a pre-populated Room in Veoci with the click of a button" ([Veoci blog](https://veoci.com/blog/virtual-emergency-operations-center-eoc-long-term-pandemic-management)). Iredell County, inside a nuclear plant's planning zone, moved its EOP out of binders into executable plans with map layers and geofencing ([Iredell](https://veoci.com/gated/nuclear-response-planning-iredell-eop)). **Custom Actions** are the automation object: automatic (email, form to workflow), manual or scheduled, with a builder that maps fields from source to target to create "work order creation, data transfers, and more" ([Level 2 boot camp](https://veoci.com/training/level-2-admin-boot-camp-advanced)). No formula syntax or scripting language was confirmed; one training page mentions how to "script and automate notifications", which may mean configuration rather than code (grade L).

### Coordination and tracking, planning and operations are configured patterns

Every coordination and tracking feature found is a pattern on the primitives. The **ICS 213RR resource request** is a form with routed approvals, automatic timestamps through fulfillment, a status dashboard and "FEMA-ready exports" ([resource requests](https://veoci.com/government/resource-requests/)); AIM Gov tracks assets with QR codes, barcodes and NFC tags ([AIM Gov](https://veoci.com/asset-inventory-management/)); damage assessment is an offline-capable mobile form plotted on a map ([damage assessment](https://veoci.com/government/damage-assessments/)); accountability is a workflow (a four-step check-in to reunification at Illinois State); rosters are built from activation check-ins. The FEMA Public Assistance package names Force Account Labor, Equipment and Materials, Rented Equipment, Materials and Contract summaries fed by equipment and rate databases ([FEMA submissions](https://veoci.com/industries/government/fema-submissions/)). No NIMS resource typing, live personnel location or personnel accountability report primitive was found. Planning and operations follow the same rule: executable plans, an IAP builder with "digital master ICS forms in FEMA templates" of which only **ICS 213RR and 214 are confirmed by number** ([IAP builder](https://veoci.com/government/iap-ics/); [ICS forms](https://veoci.com/industries/government/fema-and-ics-forms/)), after-action reports that spawn corrective-action tasks with reminders ([AAR](https://veoci.com/government/after-action-reporting/)), and a COOP process that documents essential functions, required resources and recovery locations. No operational-period cycle or plan review cadence object was found.

### Dashboards, reports and maps show Veoci's records, not a GIS

Dashboards are pages with draft and live states, laid out in sections of blocks (`BUTTON`, `FORM_ENTRIES`, `RICH_TEXT`) whose buttons create a form entry, task or workflow entry or open a list ([blocks.js](https://github.com/GreywallSoftware/vuetify-test/blob/main/veoci-web/test/unit/__mocks__/data/pages/blocks.js)); Veoci markets role-tailored dashboards fed by National Weather Service data and social media ([situational awareness](https://veoci.com/government/situational-awareness/)). **Dashboards can be public** at `veoci.com/v/p/dashboard/{token}` and `p.veoci.com/{slug}`, which is how Honolulu publishes evacuation zones and shelters. Reporting centers on configurable PDF print templates for IAPs, ICS forms and AARs plus saved filtered views ([no-code platform](https://veoci.com/no-code-platform)); CSV or Excel export, scheduled reports and BI connectors were not confirmed. Maps plot geolocated entries with icons and colors by field value, layers and filters ([resource and asset management](https://veoci.com/emergency-management/resource-asset-management/)); Veoci claims an Esri partnership ([Esri page](https://veoci.com/partners/esri/)), and the City of Stamford publishes an ArcGIS map service named "Veoci" ([Stamford GIS](https://www.stamfordgis.org/public/rest/services/Veoci/MapServer)), which supports reading customer ArcGIS services but not writing to them. No OGC, KML, drawing tools or feature-service write-back surfaced.

### The API, single sign-on and contact lists are thinly documented

Veoci's REST API v2 at `https://veoci.com/api/v2` uses **per-user personal access tokens**; first-party code reads and creates entries, posts to entry chat threads, uploads temporary files and reads solution configuration (forms, workflows, triggers, custom actions) ([veoci-ticket-mcp](https://github.com/veoci-labs/veoci-ticket-mcp); [solution mapper](https://github.com/veoci-labs/veoci-solution-mapper)). Outbound webhooks, an OpenAPI document, rate limits, service accounts and named connectors (Teams, Slack, ServiceNow, Everbridge) were not found; integrations are marketed generically and sold as services, and Veoci itself warns that a long-running integration needs time "every year for maintenance" ([Veoci blog](https://veoci.com/blog/being-a-team-player-system-integration/)). Identity evidence is a SAML claim, users identified by email, groups as a container type, invitation-based room membership, external parties "with limited permissions", and a 2023 client that handles server-side session expiry on idle return. Contact lists exist as objects with members, a Contact Picker field feeds forms, and notifications follow call trees; import, sync, device priorities and opt-out handling are undocumented.

### Notification is internal; public warning goes to someone else

Veoci's notification engine sends SMS, voice calls, email and app push, fired by hand, by plan launch or by a workflow step; it escalates "through different channels and personnel until acknowledged", follows up "on a recipient's other devices", rolls down a call tree, collects **two-way SMS check-ins and polls**, shows a live board of who has and has not responded, and keeps transcripts ([mass notification](https://veoci.com/mass-notification-emergency-notification-system/); [crisis mass notification](https://veoci.com/crisis-management/mass-notification/)). Conference calls can be started from the platform ([communication and collaboration](https://veoci.com/communication-collaboration/)). **No evidence places Veoci on FEMA's IPAWS alert-origination provider list**, the 2026 lists could not be read, and Veoci's own "Beyond Mass Notification" post frames it as the process around a mass notification system rather than a public warning tool ([Veoci blog](https://veoci.com/blog/beyond-mass-notification/)); Honolulu sends HNL Alerts through another system and links the Veoci map. Searches found **no Veoci connector for Rave, Everbridge, Genasys, Regroup, Alertus or Singlewire**, and CodeRED and AlertMedia surface only as comparison competitors ([Everbridge connector catalog](https://supportcenter.everbridge.com/hc/en-us/sections/18141921182235-Everbridge-CEM-Connectors)); PeerSpot and Gartner list those vendors as Veoci's competitors ([PeerSpot](https://www.peerspot.com/products/comparisons/rave-alert_vs_veoci)). Status for IPAWS is "unknown, leaning absent" (grade M), not confirmed.

### Mobile, VIA and Whole Community Solutions

Veoci ships native iOS (also Apple silicon Macs and visionOS) and Android apps with offline editing of form entries behind an offline PIN with lockout, photo annotation, voice-to-text and QR, barcode and NFC scanning ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/veoci/id594925365); [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.greywallsoftware.veoci&hl=en_US)); the Android app shows 10K+ installs and 50 reviews, several reporting crashes and login failures. **VIA**, marketed 2025 to 2026, summarizes rooms and threads, drafts reports and communications, builds forms, workflows, print views and saved views from prompts and extracts data from PDFs; Veoci says it "runs entirely inside Veoci's secure platform" and "inherits your existing Veoci permissions", but names no model or host ([VIA FAQ](https://veoci.com/via-ai/via-faq)). **Whole Community Solutions** is a municipal bundle "for Fire, Police, Public Works, and other municipal and public safety departments", usable standalone or combined ([capability list](https://veoci.com/downloadable/veoci-whole-community-capabilities-list/)). Every public-facing piece found reduces to one construction, an unauthenticated form feeding an internal review workflow: CERT applications at Palo Alto and Alameda ([CERT management](https://veoci.com/industries/government/cert-management-solution/)), 311 requests at Trumbull ([work orders](https://veoci.com/industries/government/work-order-management/)), non-emergency self-reports at Isle of Wight ([Isle of Wight](https://veoci.com/blog/eoc-management-isle-of-wight/)) and public forms at `veoci.com/v/p/form/<id>`. Independent customer records confirm Palo Alto's volunteer use and Trumbull's monthly and permit reports ([Palo Alto ESV manual](https://www.paloalto.gov/files/assets/public/v/1/oes/esv-sites/esv_policy_manual_and_sops_20250905.pdf); [Trumbull archive](https://www.trumbull-ct.gov/Archive.aspx?ADID=4258)). The whole-community story is inter-departmental coordination inside one government, with residents limited to submitting forms; no access and functional needs registry, VOAD coordination, re-entry permits or public lifeline page surfaced.

## Fit for a Yurok-sized EOC favors labor-saving pieces over breadth

The tables below score every Veoci capability found against six tests weighted for a small tribal or rural EOC: it must work with **one to three emergency management staff and no in-house builder**; it must work through **degraded communications** (uplink loss, power loss, SMS getting through when data does not); it must not create a **recurring per-seat cost that outlives a grant**; it must support **mutual aid with counties and the state** (guests, federation, standards, Esri where the county uses it); it must keep **tribal data under tribal control** (self-hosted, scoped partner access), which FEMA's 2022 to 2026 National Tribal Strategy frames as sovereignty and self-governance ([FEMA](https://www.fema.gov/sites/default/files/documents/fema_national-tribal-strategy_08182022.pdf)); and it must help **recover costs**. Counties and states score differently: they have GIS and IT staff, identity providers, public information programs, hundreds of users and procurement rules that ask for certifications, so they weight SAML, SCIM, Esri interchange, public dashboards and horizontal scale higher. Verdicts: **adopt** means take the Veoci behavior as is (or note that OpenEOC already has it); **adapt** means build the idea natively within the binding rules; **integrate** means run it as a separate program across a process boundary; **reject** means do not build it, with collisions detailed in the next section. OpenEOC status is **verified**, **partial** or **absent** as the baseline recorded it.

## OpenEOC already beats Veoci on interchange, offline, IPAWS, federation and price

On five axes the open project is ahead today, not after more work. **Standards**: OpenEOC speaks CAP 1.2, EDXL-DE, RM and HAVE, CoT, GeoJSON and OGC API Features natively (INV-4, F20 verified), while no Veoci evidence shows any machine-to-machine emergency standard beyond ICS forms as PDFs ([ICS forms](https://veoci.com/industries/government/fema-and-ics-forms/)). **Offline**: disconnection is an invariant (INV-3), with offline XLSForm capture, queued media, a bundled basemap and an air-gapped install, against Veoci's offline editing of form entries in its mobile app ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/veoci/id594925365)). **IPAWS**: OpenEOC has a `postCAP` connector behind a two-person send the database enforces, waiting only on credentials and an MOA, while Veoci shows no alert-origination evidence and its best-known public-sector user sends alerts from another system. **Federation**: signed, human-approved instance peering (ADR-0006) and incident participation by organization code, against Veoci's undocumented "invite people into a Room" and "network of peers" ([Veoci](https://veoci.com/veoci-communities)). **Price**: INV-1 makes viewers free, against an 850-user cap at Alameda and a reviewer's complaint that "it is hard to complete tasks without purchasing the next level of licenses" ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/150183/Veoci/reviews/)). Smaller advantages follow the same pattern: versioned board templates with additive `x_` local fields, re-convergence and Ed25519-signed regional packages (INV-5, AR2, AR4) against a vendor whose engineers apparently needed a [solution mapper](https://github.com/veoci-labs/veoci-solution-mapper) just to see what a customer's solution contains; documented approvals, due rules and escalation; HMAC webhooks and TOTP MFA; eight lifelines against the seven in Veoci's lifelines blog; and INV-8's "no session expiry into data loss" against a 2023 Veoci client written around server-side idle expiry.

### OpenEOC trails on deployments, communications reach and no-code breadth

The blunt counterweight: Veoci has run Alameda County's EOC since 2019, carries a $3.421 million Austin authorization and a statewide Oregon program, and appears in a Los Angeles after-action report; OpenEOC is a 0.9.0 evaluation build that "has not supported a real activation anywhere", has no pilot jurisdiction, offers community support only with "no paid support and no service-level agreement", and still waits on external gates for real-hardware load, a real Linux install, a screen-reader pass and a second maintainer (`docs/EVALUATOR.md`; `GOVERNANCE.md`; `RELEASE-DECISION.md`). Its notification channels have been proven only against a local relay and a fixture SMS provider. It has no voice channel, no two-way SMS, no polls, no plan object, no continuity or BIA content, no volunteer module, no SAML or SCIM, and incident templates still need code. A county evaluator comparing the two would see the deployment gap first, and nothing in this report closes it except a pilot.

## The five implementation phrases are services; an open-source project ships procedure instead

"Phased delivery. No code configuration. Validated data migration. Streamline integration. Efficient timeline." did not appear on any Veoci page or document reachable through search, so its source is unknown (possibly a proposal or sales deck). ## Conclusion

Veoci's advantage over OpenEOC is not its engine; the six primitives are ordinary, and OpenEOC's board, workflow, notification and map engines match or pass them. Its advantage is labor: packages that arrive configured, a plan object that turns an EOP into one action, boot camps, and a solutions team billed as services, sitting on real contracts and real activations. That changes what "parity" should mean for OpenEOC: the next gains come from content and procedure (starter packs, executable plans, a playbook, validated imports and measured onboarding) more than from new engine features, and the one missing primitive worth building from scratch is the executable plan. For a Yurok-sized tribe the calculation is sharper than for a county. Veoci's strengths are exactly what a thin, grant-funded staff cannot use without the vendor (a blank-slate builder, SaaS that fails with the uplink, per-user renewals), while OpenEOC's strengths (offline operation, free viewers, federation, IPAWS and self-hosting) are exactly what that staff needs; the gaps that matter most to them are two-way SMS and voice reach, cost-recovery capture and ready-made content, not public portals.

The FOUO decision costs OpenEOC the features Veoci's county customers use for public information, and Honolulu shows that cost is real for counties; a published-snapshot export would recover most of it without opening the platform, and Basho can decide that separately from any public-form question. Before any of this is used in a public comparison, the evidence needs one more pass with open egress: the FEMA provider lists, the SAVER entry, Veoci's gated capability and integration sheets, and the Texas price index would move several "M" and "not found" grades to firm answers, and a Veoci-to-OpenEOC claim built on search summaries alone would not survive a procurement reviewer.
