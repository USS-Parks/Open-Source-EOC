## Merger and crosswalk boundaries

- Cal OES marks California ESF 9 as merged into California ESF 4 and California ESF 13.
- Cal OES marks California ESF 16 as merged into California ESF 13.
- The published crosswalk lists California ESF 1 through 15 alongside federal ESF 1 through 15.
- The published crosswalk lists California ESF 16, 17, and 18 as not applicable to a federal ESF.
- The current page calls California ESF 11 “Agriculture”; the crosswalk uses “Food and Agriculture.” The workspace uses the current-page title.
- The current page calls California ESF 14 “Long Term Recovery”; the crosswalk uses “Recovery.” The workspace uses the current-page title.
- The application does not infer equivalence merely from matching numbers. Any future executable crosswalk must retain the source's published relationship language.

## Remaining evidence limit

The retained source receipt does not verify effective dates for each California ESF definition. The product exposes that limitation as a doctrine gap rather than inventing dates.

The ESF workspace uses original local interface icons. Those icons communicate function categories and do not represent official Cal OES or FEMA marks. Community Lifelines remain a separate framework and are never used to infer ESF activation.
