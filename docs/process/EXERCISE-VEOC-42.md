# Pilot Activation Exercise (VEOC-42)

This is the HSEEP-shaped design for the pilot functional exercise, the
evaluation guides mapped to facets, the data-collection plan, and the results
of the internal dry-run. The dry-run runs on the platform against the demo
activation and the platform produces its own after-action report.

## Gate: the real-user pilot needs Basho

Running the exercise on a real deployment with real users is an external
engagement. Per contract item 12, Basho authorizes any external engagement
first, and the pilot jurisdiction is Basho's selection (standing question 6,
still open). This document and the dry-run are the preparation; the live pilot
does not run until Basho authorizes it and names the jurisdiction. Nothing
here contacts anyone or stands up anything outside this repository.

## HSEEP objectives

1. **Operational Coordination.** Establish command and a common operating
   picture, and run one operational period end to end.
2. **Public Information and Warning.** Draft, approve across agencies, and
   publish an evacuation message, and issue a CAP alert.
3. **Situational Assessment.** Maintain lifelines, compose a situation report,
   and keep the briefing view current.
4. **Resource Management.** Submit, triage, and escalate a 213RR across tiers
   and track it to fulfillment.
5. **Operational Communications and Collaboration.** Stand up the incident's
   collaboration space and a meeting bridge.

## Data-collection plan

Data collection runs through the platform's own modules, not a side
spreadsheet:

- Evaluators capture observations during play on the after-action module,
  tagged strength or improvement by capability.
- The immutable chronology (VEOC-11) is the evidence record, attributed and
  time-ordered.
- At exercise end the platform composes the AAR from those observations plus
  the chronology and exports it as a PDF. This is VEOC-36 eating its own
  cooking, and it is proven in `server/src/__tests__/exercise.test.ts`.

## Dry-run results (internal, on the demo activation)

The dry-run ran the scenario against the demo dataset and the platform
produced its own AAR. It completed on platform with the AAR generated by the
platform itself.

### Findings, triaged

- **fix (done):** the accessibility touch-target gap for gloved operation was
  found and fixed in VEOC-39.
- **accept:** in-process rate limiters and single-node capacity numbers are
  accepted for the per-instance floor (see the security and capacity docs);
  the shared limiter is roadmap.
- **roadmap:** server-side pagination for months-long incidents (VEOC-38
  headroom item); a manual screen-reader pass (VEOC-39 ticket A11Y-T1).
- **blocked on Basho:** the live pilot with real users and a named
  jurisdiction (contract item 12, standing question 6).

## What the live pilot adds once authorized

When Basho authorizes it and names the jurisdiction: run this same scenario
with real staff on a real deployment, collect observations live, and let the
platform produce the pilot AAR. The mechanism is proven here; only the
authorization and the jurisdiction are outstanding.
