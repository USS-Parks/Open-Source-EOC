# Job Aid: Logistics Section Chief

In an exercise, follow the [training kit ground rules](./README.md) and mark
every entry as the exercise's rules of play say. A screen named here that is
not on the rail appears when you tick **Show every section** under
**Settings**.

## Account role and acting position

- **Account role: Member.** Labor rates and the equipment rate schedule are
  set by an administrator.
- **Acting position: Logistics Section Chief.** A request assigned to this
  position names you as its owner.

## What the position does

Sources, tracks and demobilizes resources through the ICS 213RR lifecycle,
keeps the volunteer roster, and records costs. Where no Finance/Admin
Section Chief is seated, you also keep the force account: the hours of the
jurisdiction's own staff and equipment that Public Assistance may reimburse.

## First 15 minutes

1. In the command bar choose the incident under **Selected incident**, the
   current period under **Operational period** and **Logistics Section
   Chief** under **Acting position**.
2. Check in on **Operations > Staffing**.
3. On **Operations > Resources**, read every request: state, priority,
   receiving and supplying organization, owner and next action.
4. Select **Open** on each open request to see who changed it, when and why.

## Every operational period

- **Move each request one honest step at a time.** Add a note that says what
  actually happened, then choose the step: **Accept**, **Start sourcing**,
  **Mark deployed**, **Mark fulfilled**, **Start demobilizing** or
  **Close**. **Decline** (before sourcing) and **Cancel request** (until
  deployment) ask for the reason, which the requester sees.
- **Name the owner.** A request in sourcing needs a named authority: choose a
  position under **Assign to named authority**, then **Assign and advance**.
- **Print the ICS 213RR.** In a request's details, **Print ICS 213RR** prints
  it as it stands, with the approval, supplier and costs. For the period's
  plan, the Planning Section Chief starts it as a form under **Planning > ICS
  Forms**; write the delivery location and supplier contact there.
- **Record costs.** Under **Open**, **Reimbursement costs**: category,
  **Amount (USD)**, description and the date incurred, then **Record cost**.
  **Export costs (CSV)** downloads every cost on the request with a total.
- **Keep the volunteers.** On **Operations > Volunteers**, **Roster**: **Add
  volunteer** with their credentials and expiry dates. **Deployments**: deploy
  one on the selected incident in a role, ticking the credentials the role
  needs; a missing or expired one warns first, and **Deploy anyway** keeps
  the warning on the row. End each deployment; **Hours** counts only ended
  ones.
- **Keep the force account.** On **Operations > Damage Assessment**, **Force
  account** costs labor from closed check-ins and past shifts, and equipment
  from **Record equipment hours**. Rows marked **No labor rate** or **No
  rate** need a rate from an administrator. **Download labor summary** and
  **Download equipment summary** give FEMA's summary layouts.
- **Report status.** State at the briefing which requests are deployed and
  which are still only received or accepted.

## Screens used

| Screen | Use |
|---|---|
| Operations > Resources | Lifecycle, assignment, history, costs, ICS 213RR |
| Operations > Volunteers | Roster, credentials, deployments and hours |
| Operations > Damage Assessment | Force account summaries |
| Operations > Boards | Shelters and Resource Requests boards |
| Coordination > Messages | Threads with Operations and the Liaison Officer |

## What the product does not do

- Submitted, triaged and assigned are not delivered. Do not report a resource
  as in place until the request is deployed.
- **Escalate to another tier** needs a registered peer and its token.
- The screens export costs one request at a time.
- Volunteer hours do not enter the force account, which costs paid staff
  only. Overtime is by daily threshold, not weekly.
- The product ships no FEMA equipment rates; an administrator imports the
  schedule.
