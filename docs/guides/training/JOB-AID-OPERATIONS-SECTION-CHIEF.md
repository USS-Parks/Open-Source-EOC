# Job Aid: Operations Section Chief

In an exercise, follow the [training kit ground rules](./README.md) and mark
every entry as the exercise's rules of play say. A screen named here that is
not on the rail appears when you tick **Show every section** under
**Settings**.

## Account role and acting position

- **Account role: Member.**
- **Acting position: Operations Section Chief.** Your checklist tasks are
  assigned to this position, so they appear under **My Tasks** only while you
  act in it.

## What the position does

Directs tactical response: turns field reports into operational records,
requests what the field needs, keeps shelters and radio traffic on record,
and writes or feeds the period's ICS 204 assignments.

## First 15 minutes

1. In the command bar choose the incident under **Selected incident**, the
   current period under **Operational period** and **Operations Section
   Chief** under **Acting position**.
2. Check in on **Operations > Staffing**.
3. On **Operations > Tasks**, **My Tasks**, work your checklist: **Start**,
   do the work, **Complete**. A task with an unfinished prerequisite stays
   blocked.
4. On **Operations > Resources**, read every open request: its state, owner
   and next action.
5. Open **Operations > Field Reports** and **Situation > Map** and keep both
   in view.

## Every operational period

- **Act on field reports.** Select a report on **Field Reports** to open its
  record. When it describes a road closure, add a record on the Road Closures
  board under **Operations > Boards** and tell the Situation Unit. On **Map**,
  **Add point** places a record on one of the incident's boards, even with
  no connection.
- **Keep shelters current.** Update the Shelters board: status, capacity and
  occupancy. Where the incident has the Shelter Registrations board, register
  each household there and work its **In shelter**, **Needs** and
  **Reunification** views. Record needs as what the shelter must provide,
  never a diagnosis.
- **Keep radio and runner traffic.** Where the incident has the Radio and
  Runner Log, record each message sent or received by radio, runner or
  landline; **Awaiting receipt** lists what is not yet confirmed.
- **Request what the field needs.** On **Resources**, enter the **Requested
  item**, **Quantity**, **Priority** and **Request notes**, then **Submit
  request**. Submitted is not delivered; watch the state change.
- **Coordinate by message.** On **Coordination > Messages**, start a thread
  to a position, then send. A message written with no connection shows
  **Queued on this device** and goes when the connection returns.
- **Write the ICS 204s.** On **Planning > ICS Forms**, choose the period,
  then under **Form to start** choose **ICS 204: Assignment List**, name the
  division or group and **Start form**. Write the assignment, tactics and
  resources, then **Save and mark ready**. The Planning Section Chief
  assembles the IAP from them.
- **Read what actions did.** A board may set a field, open a linked record
  or notify someone when you save. The record's **Change history** names
  each change made by an action.
- **Hand off** under your own account at shift change.

## Screens used

| Screen | Use |
|---|---|
| Operations > Field Reports | Reports from Smart Forms and field capture |
| Operations > Boards | Road Closures, Shelters, registrations, radio log, Activity Log |
| Operations > Resources | Request intake and lifecycle |
| Operations > Tasks | Checklist work for your position |
| Situation > Map | Closures and field reports; new points |
| Planning > ICS Forms | The period's ICS 204 assignments |
| Coordination > Messages | Position-to-position threads |

## What the product does not do

- A field report does not create a road closure by itself; you record it.
- Messages have no read or delivery receipts.
- Requests on **Resources** reach the IAP only as their own ICS 213RR; the
  201 and 215 resource lines read the Resource Requests board.
- A shelter registration and a hotline call are staff entries; there is no
  public form.
