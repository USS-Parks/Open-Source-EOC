# Job Aid: Operations Section Chief

Use with the [training kit ground rules](./README.md). Start every entry with
`SYNTHETIC`.

## Account role and acting position

- **Account role: Member.** The seeded account `demo-operator@example.org`
  already holds this position; the instructor may give it to a new account.
- **Acting position: Operations Section Chief.** Your checklist tasks are
  assigned to this position, so they appear under **My Tasks** only while you
  act in it.

## What the position does

Directs tactical response: turns field reports into operational records,
requests what the field needs, and gives the Planning Section Chief the
assignments for the ICS-204.

## First 15 minutes

1. Set the command bar: the synthetic incident, **OP SYNTHETIC 1**, and
   **Operations Section Chief**.
2. Check in on **Operations > Staffing**.
3. On **Operations > Tasks**, **My Tasks**, one task is already in progress.
   Work "Confirm resource status with dispatch" and "Open the resource request
   board": **Start**, do the work, **Complete**. A task with an unfinished
   prerequisite stays blocked.
4. On **Operations > Resources**, read the triaged request for synthetic
   portable generators. Its note says nothing was ordered or delivered.
5. Open **Operations > Field Reports** and **Situation > Map** and keep both
   in view.

## Every operational period

- **Act on field reports.** Select a report on **Field Reports** to open its
  record. When it describes a road closure, add a record on the Road Closures
  board under **Operations > Boards** (road, reason, status, estimated
  reopening, location) and tell the Situation Unit.
- **Keep shelter status current.** Update the Shelters board record: status,
  capacity and occupancy.
- **Request what the field needs.** On **Resources**, enter the **Requested
  item**, **Quantity**, **Priority** and **Request notes**, then **Submit
  request**. Submitted is not delivered; watch the state change.
- **Coordinate by message.** On **Coordination > Messages**, start a thread
  to a position (for example Logistics Section Chief) with a title, then send.
  Messages have no read or delivery receipts.
- **Feed the ICS-204.** Give the Planning Section Chief each assignment: its
  name, the supervising position, tactics and resources.
- **Hand off** under your own account at shift change.

## Screens used

| Screen | Use |
|---|---|
| Operations > Field Reports | Reports from Smart Forms and field capture |
| Operations > Boards | Road Closures, Shelters, Activity Log |
| Operations > Resources | Request intake and lifecycle |
| Operations > Tasks | Checklist work for your position |
| Situation > Map | Closures and field reports on the map |
| Coordination > Messages | Position-to-position threads |

## What the product does not do

- **Tasks** cannot add a task. Record new work on a board or in a message.
- A field report does not create a road closure by itself; you record it.
- Requests on **Resources** do not appear on the IAP forms; the Planning
  Section Chief needs the lines on the Resource Requests board.
- Only one organization exists in the seed, so another agency cannot accept
  an assignment.
