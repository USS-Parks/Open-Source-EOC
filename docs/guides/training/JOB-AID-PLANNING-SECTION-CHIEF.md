# Job Aid: Planning Section Chief

In an exercise, follow the [training kit ground rules](./README.md) and mark
every entry as the exercise's rules of play say. A screen named here that is
not on the rail appears when you tick **Show every section** under
**Settings**.

## Account role and acting position

- **Account role: Member.** You write the period's forms, assemble the IAP
  and submit it; the EOC Director approves it, because approval needs the
  admin role.
- **Acting position: Planning Section Chief.** Each form records its
  preparer and position, so select it before you write anything.

## What the position does

Runs the planning cycle: keeps the operational period straight, writes the
period's ICS forms and assembles the IAP from them, schedules the SITREP with
the Situation Unit, and keeps the after-action record growing.

## First 15 minutes

1. In the command bar choose the incident under **Selected incident**, the
   current period under **Operational period** and **Planning Section
   Chief** under **Acting position**.
2. Check in on **Operations > Staffing**.
3. Read **Planning > Operational Periods**: the current period, its times and
   its revision history.
4. On **Planning > ICS Forms**, choose the period under **Operational period
   revision**. **ICS forms for this period** lists the forms already started,
   with version, draft or ready, and preparer.
5. Read the incident's plan, if it came from one: on **Incident Setup**,
   **Incident plan** shows its sections and the tasks still to be released.
6. Agree the SITREP time with the Situation Unit and tell the EOC Director
   when the next period must be recorded.

## Every operational period

1. **Start each form.** Under **Form to start** choose the form, then
   **Start form**. It opens as a draft prefilled from the incident's records.
   An ICS 204, 213 or 214 asks for a name first; an ICS 213RR asks for its
   **Resource request**. A form the period already has opens instead.
2. **Fill it block by block.** Write the 202 objectives the EOC Director
   set, the 205 channels and the Safety Officer's 208 message. **Save as
   draft** keeps a version; **Save and mark ready** when the form is done.
   **Versions** lists every save; **Print version** prints the form.
3. **Assemble the IAP.** Under **Assemble the IAP from these forms** the
   202, 203, 204, 205, 205A, 206, 207 and 208 are ticked; tick the 215 or
   215A only if they belong in the printed plan. Drafts cannot be ticked.
   Select **Assemble IAP from** the forms, then **Review it in the IAP
   workspace**.
4. **Submit.** On **Planning > IAP**, choose the plan, read **Forms in this
   plan**, then **Submit for approval** and tell the EOC Director. After
   approval, **Download revision** for the PDF.
5. **Change an approved plan through its forms.** Change the form and **Save
   and mark ready**: the plan's next revision starts as a draft holding it,
   and the approved revision stays as approved. When a change is waiting, the
   IAP shows **Take the changed forms into this draft** or **Start
   revision** with the changed forms.
6. **Record observations** on **Planning > AAR** as they happen, then
   **Record observation**. When a corrective action should change a plan,
   choose it under **Plan to update** and **Plan section**; its owner is
   reminded when it falls due.
7. Optional: on **Planning > Reports**, build a report over a board, choose
   a **Chart**, and download it as PDF for the briefing.

## Screens used

| Screen | Use |
|---|---|
| Planning > Operational Periods | Current period and its history (read only for members) |
| Planning > ICS Forms | The period's forms, versions, printing and IAP assembly |
| Planning > IAP | The plan's forms, submission, revisions and PDF |
| Planning > AAR | Observations and corrective actions linked to plans |
| Planning > Reports | Tables and charts over a board, PDF and Excel |
| Incident Setup | The incident's plan and its waiting tasks |
| Situation > SITREP, Chronology | Frozen briefing, attributed record |
| Operations > Boards | Sign In/Out, Resource Requests, Activity Log |

## What the product does not do

- Prefill reads boards: the 211 reads the Sign In/Out board, the 201 and 215
  resource lines the Resource Requests board, and the 205 a radio
  communications board. Requests on **Resources** reach the plan only as
  their own ICS 213RR. Type in what the boards do not hold.
- The 206 and 208 start empty. Write them.
- A plan in approval keeps the forms it was submitted with; a change waits
  for the next revision.
- A member cannot record a new operational period or approve the IAP.
