# Job Aid: Planning Section Chief

Use with the [training kit ground rules](./README.md). Start every entry with
`SYNTHETIC`.

## Account role and acting position

- **Account role: Member.** You assemble and submit the IAP; the EOC Director
  approves it, because approval needs the admin role.
- **Acting position: Planning Section Chief.** Assembly records the preparing
  position, so select it before you build anything.

## What the position does

Runs the planning cycle: keeps the operational period straight, turns the
current picture into the IAP, schedules the SITREP with the Situation Unit,
and keeps the after-action record growing during the exercise.

## First 15 minutes

1. Set the command bar: the synthetic incident, **OP SYNTHETIC 1**, and
   **Planning Section Chief**.
2. Check in on **Operations > Staffing**.
3. Read **Planning > Operational Periods**: the current period, its times and
   its revision history.
4. On **Planning > ICS Forms**, choose the period under **Operational period
   revision** and use **Preview selected form** on ICS-201, ICS-203 and
   ICS-211. Note what is empty and why.
5. Agree the SITREP time with the Situation Unit and tell the EOC Director
   when the next period must be recorded.

## Every operational period

1. Before assembly, make sure the boards hold what the forms read: the Sign
   In/Out board for the ICS-211, the Resource Requests board for the ICS-201
   and ICS-215 resource lines, and the Activity Log for the ICS-214.
2. On **Planning > ICS Forms**, choose the period revision and **Assemble
   draft IAP**. Assembly stores a snapshot; later changes need a new
   assembly or revision.
3. **Review draft in IAP workspace.** Under the ICS-204 editor, **Add
   assignment**: a name, the **Named supervisor authority**, **Tactics, one
   per line**, and resources with **Add resource**. **Save ICS-204
   assignments**.
4. **Submit for approval.** Tell the EOC Director. After approval, use
   **Download revision** for the PDF. Changing an approved plan creates a new
   draft revision; the approved one stays unchanged.
5. Record observations on **Planning > AAR** as they happen: core
   capability, capability element, finding type, observation and
   recommendation, then **Record observation**.
6. Optional: on **Planning > Reports**, build a report over the Shelters or
   Resource Requests board and **Download PDF** for the briefing.

## Screens used

| Screen | Use |
|---|---|
| Planning > Operational Periods | Current period and its history (read only for members) |
| Planning > ICS Forms | Form preview, IAP assembly |
| Planning > IAP | ICS-204 assignments, submission, PDF |
| Planning > AAR | Observations and corrective actions |
| Planning > Reports | Saved tables over a board, PDF and Excel |
| Situation > SITREP, Chronology | Frozen briefing, attributed record |
| Operations > Boards | Sign In/Out, Resource Requests, Activity Log |

## What the product does not do

- No screen field for objectives (ICS-202) or the ICS-208 safety message.
  State the director's objectives at the briefing and note the gap.
- Requests on **Resources** do not reach the ICS-201 or ICS-215; those read
  the Resource Requests board.
- The ICS-205 needs a radio communications board, which the Wildfire incident
  does not have, so it assembles empty.
- A member cannot record a new operational period or approve the IAP.
- **Tasks** cannot add a task.
