# Job Aid: Situation Unit

Use with the [training kit ground rules](./README.md). Start every entry with
`SYNTHETIC`.

## Account role and acting position

- **Account role: Member.**
- **Acting position: Situation Unit Leader.** The standard set has no such
  position; the instructor adds it on **Administration > Positions** and
  assigns it to you before class.

## What the position does

Keeps the common operating picture honest. You collect reports, record
attributed lifeline and ESF assessments, keep unknown, stale and missing
states visible, and freeze the SITREP that everyone briefs from.

## First 15 minutes

1. Set the command bar: the synthetic incident, **OP SYNTHETIC 1**, and
   **Situation Unit Leader**.
2. Check in on **Operations > Staffing**.
3. On **Situation > ESFs & Lifelines**, **Lifelines** tab, list the state of
   each lifeline: Energy unstable, Communications unknown, Transportation
   stale, the rest not assessed. Missing or unknown never means stable.
4. On **Situation > Map**, read each layer's source, freshness and coverage.
   The synthetic road source covers only part of the incident area.
5. Open **Operations > Field Reports** and keep it open for new reports.

## Every operational period

- **Update assessments from evidence.** On the Lifelines tab select the
  lifeline, then **Update assessment**. Give the condition, confidence,
  impact statement and the evidence (cite the field report or call). The
  ESFs tab uses **Add assessment** or **Revise assessment**.
- **Resolve conflicts only by decision.** Competing current reports are
  settled by an attributed decision with a rationale, never by deleting one.
- **Keep the check-in roster for the IAP.** The IAP's ICS-211 reads the Sign
  In/Out board, not Staffing. Before the Planning Section Chief assembles,
  add a Sign In/Out record for each checked-in player.
- **Freeze the SITREP.** On **Situation > SITREP**, **Compose and freeze**.
  Check the incident, period and source warnings before you announce it.
- **Read the chronology.** On **Situation > Chronology**, **Significant
  events** shows the milestones; **All events** shows everything you may
  read. Correct a wrong entry with **Add correction**.

## Screens used

| Screen | Use |
|---|---|
| Situation > Overview, Map | Picture, layers, freshness and coverage |
| Situation > ESFs & Lifelines | Attributed assessments and history |
| Situation > SITREP | Compose and freeze |
| Situation > Chronology | Attributed, append-only record |
| Operations > Field Reports | Incoming field reports |
| Operations > Boards | Road Closures, Shelters, Sign In/Out |
| Planning > Reports | Optional status tables for the briefing |

## What the product does not do

- A lifeline condition is never derived from counts or ESF activation. You
  record it.
- A frozen SITREP does not change; compose a new one for new facts.
- Staffing check-ins do not reach the IAP's ICS-211.
- Only one organization exists in the seed, so assessments from partner
  agencies cannot be played.
