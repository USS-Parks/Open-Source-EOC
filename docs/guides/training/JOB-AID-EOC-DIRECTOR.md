# Job Aid: EOC Director

Use with the [training kit ground rules](./README.md). Start every entry with
`SYNTHETIC`.

## Account role and acting position

- **Account role: Admin.** Only an administrator can approve an IAP, record a
  new operational period and change task details. An admin account enrolls an
  authenticator app at its first sign-in.
- **Acting position: Incident Commander.** The standard set has no EOC
  Director position; Incident Commander carries the command checklist.

## What the position does

Sets priorities and objectives, approves the plan, public information and
local alerts, and opens each operational period. Each approval is a server
action recorded under your name and acting position.

## First 15 minutes

1. In the command bar choose **SYNTHETIC Ridge Wildfire Exercise** under
   Selected incident, **OP SYNTHETIC 1** under Operational period and
   **Incident Commander** under Acting position.
2. Check in on **Operations > Staffing**.
3. Read **Situation > Overview**, **Map** and **ESFs & Lifelines**. Name
   which lifelines are unknown, stale or not assessed.
4. On **Operations > Tasks**, **My Tasks**, start "Assume command and
   announce on the significant events board". Add a Significant Events record
   under **Operations > Boards**, then complete the task.
5. State the initial objectives aloud. Record them as a Significant Events
   record, since the IAP has no objectives field, then complete "Set initial
   incident objectives".

## Every operational period

- **Open the period.** On **Planning > Operational Periods**, enter the
  period label, start, end and a reason for revision, then **Save area
  revision**. The new period becomes current. Tell every player to select it.
  Then complete "Establish the operational period".
- **Brief from a frozen SITREP** under **Situation > SITREP**. Read its
  unknown, stale and missing labels aloud.
- **Approve the IAP.** On **Planning > IAP**, select the plan, read the
  stored forms and ICS-204 assignments, then **Approve revision**. Use
  **Download revision** for the PDF.
- **Approve public information.** On **Coordination > JIC**, open the frozen
  SITREP, find the release under **Waiting for review**, select **Review**
  and **Approve for** the named agency. Your panel then offers **Publish
  release** and **Answer with approved release**; the drafter's panel does
  not show your decision. Publish to the public information feed only, and
  answer the media inquiry the PIO logged.
- **Approve local alerts.** In the right-dock Notifications section select
  **Open center**, select the record in review and **Approve local alert**.
  Approval transmits nothing.
- **Hand off** under your own account, as the operator quickstart describes.

## Screens used

| Screen | Use |
|---|---|
| Situation > Overview, Map, ESFs & Lifelines | Current picture before each decision |
| Situation > SITREP | Frozen briefing |
| Situation > Chronology | Who did what, under which position |
| Operations > Tasks, Boards, Staffing | Command checklist, Significant Events, check-in |
| Planning > Operational Periods, IAP | New period, plan approval |
| Coordination > JIC | Release approval, publication, inquiry answer |
| Notifications > Open center | Local alert approval |

## What the product does not do

- No screen field for objectives or the ICS-208 safety message, so an
  approved IAP carries neither.
- One current operational period per incident; a new one replaces it.
- **Tasks** cannot add a task. New work goes on a board or a message.
- An approved CAP record is local. The demo has no IPAWS connection.
- One organization in the seed: no partner agency can review a release from
  its own instance.
