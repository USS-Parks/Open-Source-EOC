# Job Aid: EOC Director

In an exercise, follow the [training kit ground rules](./README.md) and mark
every entry as the exercise's rules of play say. A screen named here that is
not on the rail appears when you tick **Show every section** under
**Settings**.

## Account role and acting position

- **Account role: Admin.** Only an administrator can approve an IAP, record a
  new operational period, activate a plan and decide late submissions. An
  admin account enrolls an authenticator app at its first sign-in.
- **Acting position: Incident Commander.** The standard set has no EOC
  Director position; Incident Commander carries the command checklist.

## What the position does

Sets priorities and objectives, approves the plan, public information and
local alerts, and opens each operational period. Each approval is a server
action recorded under your name and acting position.

## First 15 minutes

1. In the command bar choose the incident under **Selected incident**, the
   current period under **Operational period** and **Incident Commander**
   under **Acting position**.
2. Check in on **Operations > Staffing**.
3. Read **Situation > Overview**, **Map** and **ESFs & Lifelines**. Name
   which lifelines are unknown, stale or not assessed.
4. On **Operations > Tasks**, **My Tasks**, work the command checklist:
   **Start**, do the work, **Complete**. A plan's timed tasks arrive here when
   their time comes.
5. State the initial objectives aloud. The Planning Section Chief writes them
   on the period's ICS 202 under **Planning > ICS Forms**.

## Every operational period

- **Open the period.** On **Planning > Operational Periods**, enter the
  period label, start, end and a reason for revision, then **Save area
  revision**. The new period becomes current. Tell every player to select it.
- **Brief from a frozen SITREP** under **Situation > SITREP**. Read its
  unknown, stale and missing labels aloud.
- **Approve the IAP.** On **Planning > IAP**, choose the plan, read **Forms
  in this plan** and each stored form, then **Approve revision**. Use
  **Download revision** for the PDF. A form changed after approval starts
  the plan's next revision as a draft; the approved one stays as approved.
- **Approve public information.** On **Coordination > JIC**, open the frozen
  SITREP, find the release under **Waiting for review**, select **Review**
  and **Approve for** the named agency. Your panel then offers **Publish
  release** and **Answer with approved release**; the drafter's panel does
  not show your decision.
- **Approve local alerts.** Select the **Notifications** bell in the command
  bar, then **Open center**. Under **Alert records** select the record in
  review and **Approve local alert**. Approval transmits nothing.
- **Decide late submissions.** Work that reached a closed incident waits
  under **Late submissions** on **Incident Setup**. **Refuse** it with a
  reason, or reopen the incident and **Accept** it; it is applied as its
  sender.
- **Hand off** under your own account, as the operator quickstart describes.

## Activate from a plan

On **Data and administration > Incident Setup**, **Plans** lists the
jurisdiction's plans. Select **Activate** on the plan, enter the **Incident
name**, then **Activate the plan**. The report says how many tasks were
released and whom the notice reached; **Switch to** moves the console to the
new incident. Later tasks are released to their positions on time. A
continuity plan also opens a task for each essential function, due within
its recovery time.

## Screens used

| Screen | Use |
|---|---|
| Situation > Overview, Map, ESFs & Lifelines | Current picture before each decision |
| Situation > SITREP, Chronology | Frozen briefing; who did what, under which position |
| Operations > Tasks, Boards, Staffing | Command checklist, Significant Events, check-in |
| Planning > Operational Periods, IAP | New period, plan approval |
| Coordination > JIC | Release approval, publication, inquiry answer |
| Notifications (the bell, Open center) | Local alert approval |
| Incident Setup | Plans and their activation, late submissions |

## What the product does not do

- One current operational period per incident; a new one replaces it.
- An approved CAP record is local; nothing reaches the public from approval.
- A plan does not activate itself on a date; a recurring event plan is
  activated for each occurrence.
- A late submission is accepted only while the incident is open.
