# Exercise Facilitator Guide: Deerhorn Lightning Complex

For the lead facilitator, controllers and evaluators. Players receive the
[situation manual](./EXERCISE-SITUATION-MANUAL.md), not this guide. The
package follows the shape of an HSEEP exercise and is not certified or
endorsed by FEMA or any other authority.

## Where the evidence is

- **Situation > Chronology**: attributed, append-only record of milestones.
- **Boards**: Significant Events, Road Closures, Shelters, Field Reports,
  Incident Facilities, and the Cultural resources board outside the incident.
- **Operations > Resources**: each request's owner, stage and history.
- **Situation > ESFs & Lifelines**: each lifeline's assessment history.
- **Coordination > JIC**: the joint release and its decisions.
- The alerts center (from the bell): each government's exercise alerts.
- **Coordination > Messages**: the Unified command thread and the Cultural
  resources thread.

## Product limits players will meet

State these at the pre-exercise briefing so a limit is recorded, not argued:

- A partner cannot record its own decision on the owner's joint release; the
  owner's Public Information Officer records it as stated.
- A partner's alert lives in the partner's jurisdiction; the owner does not
  see it.
- An incident has one owning organization in the data model; unified command
  is represented by the Yurok Tribe's coordinator role.
