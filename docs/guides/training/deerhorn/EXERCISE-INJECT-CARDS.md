# Exercise Inject Cards: Deerhorn Lightning Complex

One card per inject of the [situation manual](./EXERCISE-SITUATION-MANUAL.md),
for controllers and simulators to hand out or read aloud. Print this page and
cut along the rules. The manual is the source: if a card and the manual
differ, the manual wins.

Each card has a line for the controller to note when it was delivered and who
acknowledged it.
