# Job Aid: Public Information Officer

Use with the [training kit ground rules](./README.md). Start every entry with
`SYNTHETIC`.

## Account role and acting position

- **Account role: Member.** You draft and submit; the EOC Director records
  the approval.
- **Acting position: Public Information Officer.** Your two checklist tasks
  are assigned to this position.

## What the position does

Prepares accurate, approved public information from the frozen SITREP, logs
and answers media inquiries with approved language only, and prepares local
alert text for review. Nothing in the demo reaches the public.

## First 15 minutes

1. Set the command bar: the synthetic incident, **OP SYNTHETIC 1**, and
   **Public Information Officer**.
2. Check in on **Operations > Staffing**.
3. On **Operations > Tasks**, **My Tasks**: "Draft the initial public
   statement" and "Confirm media contact roster". **Start** each, and
   **Complete** it only when the work is done.
4. Read **Coordination > Contacts**: the synthetic contacts the instructor
   loaded.
5. In the right-dock Notifications section, select **Open center** and read
   the seeded exercise CAP record. It was never transmitted.

## Every operational period

1. **Wait for the frozen SITREP.** On **Coordination > JIC**, open the
   latest frozen SITREP. Leaving it clears the panel.
2. **Log inquiries first.** Under **Media inquiries**, enter the **Media
   outlet**, **Inquiry subject** and **Question**, then **Log inquiry**.
   **Assign** it to your position.
3. **Draft the release.** Edit the **Release title** and **Draft statement**.
   Under **Required reviewing agencies** enter `County OES`. **Save JIC
   draft**, then **Submit for review**. A saved draft that is not submitted is
   not listed anywhere, so submit before you leave the page.
4. **Do not approve your own release.** Your panel also shows **Approve for
   County OES**; leave it to the EOC Director. Your panel does not refresh with
   the director's decision. Publication to the public information feed and the
   answer to the inquiry happen in the director's panel.
5. **Prepare a local alert.** In the notification center, **Compose local
   alert**: **Record type** Exercise, sender, event, headline, description,
   instruction, urgency, severity and certainty. **Review local draft**, then
   **Save local draft**, then **Submit for local review**. The director
   approves it.

## Screens used

| Screen | Use |
|---|---|
| Coordination > JIC | Release draft, submission, inquiries, public feed |
| Situation > SITREP | The frozen briefing the release comes from |
| Notifications > Open center | Local CAP draft, exercise and review |
| Coordination > Contacts | Media and partner contacts |
| Operations > Tasks | PIO checklist |

## What the product does not do

- No external send. The compose drawer shows **External send unavailable**,
  and approval transmits nothing.
- The demo has no collaboration channels; publish to the public information
  feed only.
- Only one organization exists in the seed, so no partner agency reviews from
  its own instance.
- An answer cites approved or published language only; there is no free-text
  reply.
