# Job Aid: Public Information Officer

In an exercise, follow the [training kit ground rules](./README.md) and mark
every entry as the exercise's rules of play say. A screen named here that is
not on the rail appears when you tick **Show every section** under
**Settings**.

## Account role and acting position

- **Account role: Member.** You draft and submit; the EOC Director records
  the approval.
- **Acting position: Public Information Officer.** Your checklist tasks are
  assigned to this position.

## What the position does

Prepares accurate, approved public information from the frozen SITREP, logs
and answers media inquiries with approved language only, keeps track of
public calls and rumors, and prepares local alert text for review.

## First 15 minutes

1. In the command bar choose the incident under **Selected incident**, the
   current period under **Operational period** and **Public Information
   Officer** under **Acting position**.
2. Check in on **Operations > Staffing**.
3. On **Operations > Tasks**, **My Tasks**, **Start** each task, and
   **Complete** it only when the work is done.
4. Read **Coordination > Contacts** for media and partner contacts.
5. Select the **Notifications** bell in the command bar, then **Open
   center**, and read the **Alert records** already there.

## Every operational period

1. **Wait for the frozen SITREP.** On **Coordination > JIC**, open the
   latest frozen SITREP. Leaving it clears the panel.
2. **Log inquiries first.** Under **Media inquiries**, enter the **Media
   outlet**, **Inquiry subject** and **Question**, then **Log inquiry**.
   **Assign** it to your position.
3. **Draft the release.** Edit the **Release title** and **Draft statement**.
   Under **Required reviewing agencies** enter each agency that must approve.
   **Save JIC draft**, then **Submit for review**. A saved draft that is not
   submitted is not listed anywhere, so submit before you leave the page.
4. **Do not approve your own release.** Leave the approval to the EOC
   Director. Your panel does not refresh with the director's decision;
   publication and the answer to the inquiry happen in the director's panel.
5. **Keep the hotline.** Where the incident has the Hotline and Inquiry Log
   board, log each call from the public there: topic, question and the
   answer given. Work its **Needs follow-up** view each period, and answer
   what **Rumors** collects with approved language. A call marked escalated
   notifies the Operations Section Chief.
6. **Prepare a local alert.** In the notification center, **Compose local
   alert**: **Record type**, sender, event, headline, description,
   instruction, urgency, severity and certainty. **Review local draft**, then
   **Save local draft**, then **Submit for local review**. The director
   approves it.

## Screens used

| Screen | Use |
|---|---|
| Coordination > JIC | Release draft, submission, inquiries, public feed |
| Situation > SITREP | The frozen briefing the release comes from |
| Notifications (the bell, Open center) | Local CAP draft, exercise and review |
| Operations > Boards | Hotline and Inquiry Log |
| Coordination > Contacts | Media and partner contacts |
| Operations > Tasks | PIO checklist |

## What the product does not do

- No external send from the compose drawer: it shows **External send
  unavailable**, and approval transmits nothing.
- An answer cites approved or published language only; there is no free-text
  reply.
- The hotline log is entered by staff; there is no public form. A caller's
  name, number and words are left out of notifications.
