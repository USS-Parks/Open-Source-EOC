# Incident participation

Open Incidents, choose Participants for the incident, then add each person
needed for the response. Enter the registered organization code, existing
account email, incident position, role, expiry and reason. The person must
belong to that organization. No organization type is mandatory. Geography
does not enroll an organization; participation does not establish unified
command. The sponsoring organization keeps its existing member access.

Read-only participants can inspect the incident and its operational area.
Coordinators can also revise the operational area. Contributor authority is
the seam used by the following workspace integration prompt, VEOC-79B. Only
sponsoring-organization administrators manage participation or close incidents.
Participation does not grant organization-wide access to boards or private
records. Shared incident workspace integration is tracked separately.

Expiry uses the displayed local timezone. End participation with a reason
to revoke it immediately; history is retained. Revoked or expired grants
do not authorize new API or database reads or writes. A person from an
unselected organization, or another member of a selected organization who
was not individually granted access, gains no incident access.

Partner area revisions preserve the authenticated person, home organization
and incident position. Grants and revocations are audited. Existing staff
position sign-in is not replaced or reassigned by participation.

New organizations and their personnel must first be registered. Activation-time
onboarding without redeployment is VEOC-79C. The broader selected incident
workspace and record-sharing enforcement are VEOC-79B, followed by the
integrated cross-boundary exercise at VEOC-79D.
